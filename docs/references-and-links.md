**Idea raised:** a **moving video hero** might work, like on the Yasir Qadhi
site.

> ⚠️ Note the tension with the brief: the creative brief lists *heavy
> animations* and *an overlong credential-led hero* under **Avoid**, and asks
> for a **portrait-led** hero. A moving video hero should be treated as an
> open decision for the client, not an assumption — a restrained ambient loop
> is the brief-safe version. See [`AGENTS.md`](../AGENTS.md) → *Open decisions*.

## Design references (from the brief)

The brief also names three design references (see
[`creative-brief.md`](./creative-brief.md) → *03 · Design references*):

- **Omar Suleiman** — navigation, clear routes into content and booking.
- **Marginalia** — article typography, notes and reading experience.
- **The Lit Window** — selective light/dark treatment, *not* an entire site theme.

## Provenance

The site was first staged in a private organisation repo, then moved to this
public repo so GitHub Pages could host it (Pages needs a paid plan for private
repos). Directions 1–3 were imported from Jamil's `hamzatzortzis-mockups`;
direction 4 (`disciplined-thinker/`) was built here to the creative brief.
