## The Disciplined Thinker

Author · educator · founder · practitioner. An editorial personal site, not a generic speaker template.

---

## 01 · Brand system

Calm, intellectual, grounded, masculine and human. Photography-led, generously spaced, with book-like editorial detail for long-form reading.

### Colour palette

| Name | Hex |
|------|-----|
| Ivory | `#F5F2EA` |
| Ink | `#20231F` |
| Olive | `#35483E` |
| Copper | `#A46E4C` |

### Typography

- **Display:** refined literary serif (e.g. Fraunces / Source Serif 4)
- **UI:** Inter
- **Arabic:** readable Naskh family, properly typeset
- Use a restrained HT monogram only if needed.

## 02 · Content & navigation

Navigation: **About | Writing | Books | Talks | Organisations | Contact**

Articles live on-site with categories, search, reading time and references. BJJ belongs in "Practice & discipline" within About unless Hamza wants a larger section.

## 03 · Design references

- **Omar Suleiman** — navigation, clear routes into content and booking.
- **Marginalia** — article typography, notes and reading experience.
- **The Lit Window** — selective light/dark treatment, **not an entire site theme**.

## 04 · Avoid

- Generic green-and-gold Islamic motifs
- "Alpha-male" black/gold
- Oversized BJJ imagery
- Heavy animations
- Cluttered video grids
- A vague "Works" label
- An overlong, credential-led hero

## 05 · Homepage wireframe

1. **Header** — Wordmark + six navigation links
2. **Hero** — Portrait (45%) | Name, positioning, Read / Watch
3. **Featured world** — Books and key initiatives in a restrained strip
4. **Selected writing** — Three editorial article previews + View all
5. **Books** — Three book covers; current book featured
6. **Talks** — Three selected videos: talk, discussion, debate
7. **Organisations** — Sapience, Lighthouse, Visionaries etc.
8. **Practice & discipline** — BJJ documentary image + short reflection
9. **About / Contact** — Short bio, speaking/media enquiry, footer

CMS-driven articles, books and talks; SEO redirects from existing URLs where possible; mobile-first reading design; accessible contrast and fast loading.
